Built for Omarchy

Your photos,
as good-looking as your desktop.

Double-click a picture or video and it opens in a viewer that follows your Omarchy theme, with the rest of the folder an arrow key away. Press Enter for the library, where your PDFs, screenshots and recordings are too.

Free and open source, for x86_64 and ARM64 on Omarchy and Arch Linux.

# install (Omarchy edge channel)
sudo pacman -S omaroll
# open pictures and videos with Omaroll
omaroll --set-default

Quick start

  1. Install Omaroll. On Omarchy's edge channel it is in the official repository: sudo pacman -S omaroll. On other channels and plain Arch Linux, one command adds the signed repository and installs it:

    curl -fsSL https://pkgs.btso.dev/install.sh | bash -s -- omaroll

    Updates then arrive with your normal system update (omarchy update on Omarchy, sudo pacman -Syu on plain Arch).

  2. Make it the default for pictures and videos. It prints what changed, and omaroll --unset-default puts the previous apps back.

    omaroll --set-default

  3. Double-click a picture or video, or run omaroll photo.jpg. Run omaroll on its own for the library.

  4. Press ? or F1 in the library or the viewer for its shortcuts.

  5. Optional: keep pictures at full opacity and float the viewer with Omaroll's Hyprland rules. omaroll --remove-window-rules takes them out, and Settings has the same switch.

    omaroll --install-window-rules

01

Opens instantly

Pictures and videos open in one viewer. Arrow through the folder, zoom into huge photos, step video a frame at a time.

02

Feels native

Follows your Omarchy palette, font, transparency and theme changes live, in the viewer and the library.

03

Leaves files alone

Nothing is imported or copied. Albums, tags and ratings live in Omaroll, and corrections save new copies.
